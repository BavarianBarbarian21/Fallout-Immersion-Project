# FIP 1.0 – Ownership matrix

This matrix defines primary content ownership. A package may appear in a combination owned by another module, but one concrete patch must exist in only one FIP module.

## Arktos – Immersive Biomes

- Vanilla Plants Expanded and its More Plants, Mushrooms and Succulents modules
- Vanilla Animals Expanded, Royal Animals and Waste Animals
- Vanilla Fishing Expanded and Fishing Treasures
- Ancient Urban Ruins
- Vanilla Landmarks Expanded
- biome/nature combinations with Odyssey, Biotech and optional FCP content

## Corvega – Immersive Transportation

- Vehicle Framework
- Vanilla Vehicles Expanded
- Vanilla Vehicles Expanded – Tier 3
- Vanilla Vehicles Expanded – Upgrades

## Donaustahl – Immersive RimWorld

- RimWorld Core and optional vanilla DLC flavor that has no more specific owner
- Vanilla Books Expanded
- Vanilla Skills Expanded as a general system
- Vanilla Aspirations, Backstories, Events, Social Interactions and Traits Expanded
- Vanilla Hair Expanded
- Vanilla Textures Expanded and Variations
- Vanilla Outposts Expanded and its Additional Outposts, Power Grid, Delivery Logistics and Prisoner Patch modules
- general Plasteel-to-Saturnite terminology

## Future-Tec – Immersive Quests

- Vanilla Quests Expanded – Ancients
- Vanilla Quests Expanded – Cryptoforge
- Vanilla Quests Expanded – Deadlife
- Vanilla Quests Expanded – The Generator

## Greenway – Immersive Ideology

- RimWorld Ideology
- Vanilla Ideology Expanded – Dryads
- Vanilla Ideology Expanded – Hats and Rags
- Vanilla Ideology Expanded – Memes and Structures
- complete ideology combinations with Books, Cooking, Royalty and other optional sources

## H&H Tools – Immersive Factions

- shared FIP factions and faction equipment profiles
- Vanilla Base Generation Expanded
- Vanilla Factions Expanded – Medieval 2
- Vanilla Factions Expanded – Tribals
- Vanilla Factions Expanded – Settlers
- Vanilla Apparel Expanded – Accessories as faction equipment
- Vanilla Weapons Expanded – Tribal as faction equipment
- optional FCP faction/equipment integrations
- all former Sunset Medieval 2, Tribals and Settlers content

## Hubris – Immersive Psycasts

- Vanilla Psycasts Expanded
- Hemosage
- Puppeteer
- optional Royalty psycast integration without a Royalty requirement

## Lucky 38 – Immersive Hospitality

- Hospitality, Casino, Spa and Storefront
- Gastronomy, Cash Register, Therapy, Mechanoid Waiter and Vending Machines
- Dubs Bad Hygiene hospitality integrations
- Vanilla Cooking, Brewing and Food Variety Expanded
- Small Hot Spring and Spaceports
- Vanilla Trading Expanded hospitality/economy integration
- complete RobCo service-mechanoid combinations

## Poseidon – Immersive Energy

- Vanilla Furniture Expanded and its Architect, Art, Factory, Farming, Medical, Power, Production, Props and Decor, Security and Spacer modules
- Vanilla Chemfuel Expanded
- Vanilla Helixien Gas Expanded
- Vanilla Recycling Expanded
- Vanilla Temperature Expanded
- Vanilla Nutrient Paste Expanded
- optional Anomaly and Royalty energy/infrastructure integration

## Repconn – Immersive Gravships

- RimWorld Odyssey
- Vanilla Gravship Expanded
- complete Gravship combinations with Biotech, Ideology, Royalty and Skills

## RobCo – Immersive Mechanoids

- RimWorld Biotech mechanoids and mechanitors
- robots and synthetic pawn mechanics
- optional Royalty integration

## Big MT – Immersive Anomalies

- RimWorld Anomaly
- Vanilla Anomaly Expanded – Insanity
- complete Big MT combinations with WestTek and H&H Tools
- Anomaly equipment/faction integrations migrated from H&H Tools

## WestTek – Immersive Xenotypes

- RimWorld Biotech genes and xenotypes
- Vanilla Genetics Expanded
- Vanilla Races Expanded – Sanguophage
- mutant, FEV, Nightkin and Super Mutant systems
- optional Anomaly, Odyssey, Royalty, Greenway, Cooking and FCP Ghoul combinations

## Whitespring – Immersive Royalty

- RimWorld Royalty
- Vanilla Factions Expanded – Empire
- Vanilla Factions Expanded – Deserters
- Vanilla Persona Weapons Expanded
- all normal and Donaustahl/Saturnite Empire variants
- optional WestTek, H&H Tools, FCP Brotherhood and FCP Enclave combinations

## Libraries and exclusions

- Harmony: technical patch library; no FIP content ownership.
- Vanilla Expanded Framework: technical framework; no FIP content ownership merely from API use.
- FCP modules: optional sources, not FIP-owned products.
- Anthrosonae: inspiration/reference only.
- Artefacts Expanded: not a primary ownership target.
- Combat Extended family: outside the FIP refactor.

## Combination ownership examples

| Combination | Sole owner | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanoid Waiter + RobCo | Lucky 38 | service/hospitality behavior |
| Gravship + Skills | Repconn | Gravship expertise becomes Hellion expertise |
| Memes + Books | Greenway | the modified target is an Ideology meme |
| Empire + Donaustahl | Whitespring | Empire terminology with a Saturnite variant |
| Medieval 2/Settlers/Tribals + FCP Animals | H&H Tools | shared faction integration inherited from Sunset |
| Anomaly + WestTek | Big MT | containable Big MT experiments using WestTek content |
