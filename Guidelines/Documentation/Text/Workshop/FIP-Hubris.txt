[h1]Fallout Immersion Project - Hubris[/h1]
[i]"The truth is out there. It may be staring back."[/i]

[url=https://ko-fi.com/misterhouse][img]https://i.imgur.com/YUpX4Up.png[/img][/url]

[b][Open Beta][/b] This mod is under active development - updates and patches may drop anytime.
Your feedback directly shapes development!

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Cryptid conversion:[/b] rewrites Royalty's psychic presentation into Fallout-style occult research, cryptid encounters, forbidden knowledge, and wasteland revelation.
[*] [b]Renamed progression:[/b] [b]psylink[/b] becomes [b]cryptid insight[/b], [b]psyfocus[/b] becomes [b]cryptid focus[/b], [b]psycasts[/b] become [b]revelations[/b], and psycasters are presented as [b]occultists[/b].
[*] [b]Mothman ritual imagery:[/b] converts the anima tree into a [b]Mothman totem[/b] and anima grass into [b]Mothman eggs[/b], including custom replacement textures for both.
[*] [b]Royalty text pass:[/b] updates abilities, quests, research, hediffs, traits, apparel references, meditation language, alerts, and interface text tied to psychic content.
[*] [b]Vanilla Psycasts Expanded support:[/b] conditionally rewrites its schools, paths, roles, abilities, and terminology into themed identities such as High Priest, Blacksite Savant, and Doomwatcher.
[*] [b]Mechanics scope:[/b] psychic entropy, focus, meditation, ability acquisition, and anima-tree interaction retain their original gameplay behavior beneath the new fiction.
[*] [b]Visual scope:[/b] the Mothman totem and egg replacements are the module's deliberate in-game retextures; the wider conversion is textual.
[/list]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Royalty DLC[/b] - Required for psylinks, psyfocus, psycasts, meditation, and anima content.
[/list]

[h2]Recommended[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Vanilla Psycasts Expanded[/b] - Optional, with an extensive conditional occult terminology pass.
[*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3665532846][b]FIP - H&H Tools[/b][/url] - Recommended for the wider faction and cultural framework.
[/list]

[h2]Development Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*] Hubris changes presentation and terminology while intentionally preserving the underlying Royalty and Vanilla Psycasts Expanded mechanics.
[*] The Vanilla Psycasts Expanded patch is inactive when that mod is not loaded.
[*] Some names cached in existing pawns, quests, or generated content may update only when new content is produced.
[*] [url=https://github.com/BavarianBarbarian21/Fallout-Immersion-Project]Source code and development repository[/url]
[*] This mod is part of the Fallout Immersion Project (FIP) - a modular overhaul inspired by Fallout's lore. FIP is not affiliated with the Fallout Collaboration Project (FCP), but is designed to complement it. [url=https://discordapp.com/channels/644497305650593793/1414315188483657869]FIP channel in the FCP Discord[/url]
[/list]
