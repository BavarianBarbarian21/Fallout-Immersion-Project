[i]"Holy Mothman, shepherd me to the light." - Mothman cultist[/i]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Cryptid conversion:[/b] rewrites Royalty's psychic presentation into Fallout-style occult research, cryptid encounters, forbidden knowledge, and wasteland revelation.
[*] [b]Renamed progression:[/b] [b]psylink[/b] becomes [b]cryptid insight[/b], [b]psyfocus[/b] becomes [b]cryptid focus[/b], [b]psycasts[/b] become [b]revelations[/b], and psycasters are presented as [b]occultists[/b].
[*] [b]Mothman ritual imagery:[/b] converts the anima tree into a [b]Mothman totem[/b] and anima grass into [b]Mothman eggs[/b], including custom replacement textures for both.
[*] [b]Royalty text pass:[/b] updates abilities, quests, research, hediffs, traits, apparel references, meditation language, alerts, and interface text tied to psychic content.
[*] [b]Vanilla Psycasts Expanded support:[/b] conditionally rewrites its schools, paths, roles, abilities, and terminology into themed identities such as High Priest, Blacksite Savant, and Doomwatcher.
[*] [b]Mechanics scope:[/b] psychic entropy, focus, meditation, ability acquisition, and anima-tree interaction retain their original gameplay behavior beneath the new fiction.
[*] [b]Visual scope:[/b] the Mothman totem and egg replacements are the module's deliberate in-game retextures; the wider conversion is textual.
[/list]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Royalty DLC[/b] - Required for psylinks, psyfocus, psycasts, meditation, and anima content.
[/list]

[h2]Fallout Immersion Project[/h2]
FIP is a modular RimWorld overhaul inspired by Fallout. Install only the parts you want, or combine them for the complete conversion. FIP is independent from the Fallout Collaboration Project, but is built to work alongside it.
