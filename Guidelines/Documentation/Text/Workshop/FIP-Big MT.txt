[h1]Fallout Immersion Project - Big MT[/h1]
[i]"The problems began to outpace the solutions." - Old World Blues[/i]

[url=https://ko-fi.com/misterhouse][img]https://i.imgur.com/YUpX4Up.png[/img][/url]

Big MT recasts Anomaly as the aftermath of pre-War experiments that were never shut down. The monolith becomes a broadcast antenna, entities become escaped experiments, and the strange materials and phenomena of Anomaly are folded into FEV, Saturnite, ZAX and Think Tank science.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Anomaly becomes Big MT:[/b] the monolith storyline is reframed around a pre-War Big MT antenna whose strengthening signal draws failed experiments toward the colony.
[*] [b]Fallout creatures in place of abstract entities:[/b] face Nightkin, Roboscorpions, Nightstalkers, Centaurs, Trauma Harnesses, Lobotomites, Behemoths, FEV hybrids and rogue Think Tank brains.
[*] [b]FEV disasters:[/b] death pall becomes an airborne FEV release, blood rain becomes diluted mutagenic fallout, fleshbeasts become runaway FEV biomass, and the fleshmass nucleus becomes a ZAX core still carrying out its research directives.
[*] [b]Big MT artifacts and materials:[/b] bioferrite becomes Saturnite alloy, the golden cube becomes the obsessive Toaster, obelisks become experimental emitters and ZAX nodes, and ritual structures become Big MT installations.
[*] [b]West Tek crossover:[/b] with West Tek installed, escaped experiments form their own permanently hostile faction and add capturable crazed Super Mutants and stealth-field Nightkin to Anomaly encounters and study progression.
[*] [b]Cult and equipment integration:[/b] Anomaly cultists become Holy Mothman devotees, while supported weapons and apparel receive faction equipment tags that keep generated enemies consistent with the wider FIP setting.
[/list]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*] None. Anomaly is optional, but it provides the main storyline and entities that Big MT converts.
[/list]

[h2]Fallout Immersion Project[/h2]
FIP is a modular RimWorld overhaul inspired by Fallout. Install only the parts you want, or combine them for the complete conversion. FIP is independent from the Fallout Collaboration Project, but is built to work alongside it.
