[h1]Fallout Immersion Project - Corvega[/h1]
[i]"Nothing can stop a Highwayman." - Chryslus Motors[/i]

[url=https://ko-fi.com/misterhouse][img]https://i.imgur.com/YUpX4Up.png[/img][/url]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Fallout vehicle branding:[/b] converts supported Vanilla Vehicles Expanded cars into [b]Chryslus[/b] and [b]Corvega[/b] models while preserving their original vehicle roles.
[*] [b]Vehicle renames:[/b] Traveller becomes [b]Chryslus Highwayman[/b], Highwayman becomes [b]Corvega APC[/b], and Prowler, Roadkill, Roadrunner, and Lightning become their Corvega-branded counterparts.
[*] [b]Upgrade terminology:[/b] changes only two strongly Fallout-specific labels: [b]Supercar engine[/b] becomes [b]Chryslus sport engine[/b], and the Highwayman's [b]Reinforced bumper[/b] becomes a [b]brahmincatcher bumper[/b].
[*] [b]Supporting text:[/b] updates the supported vehicle labels and descriptions while leaving generic upgrade terminology unchanged.
[*] [b]Mechanics scope:[/b] speed, fuel use, capacity, armor, weapons, construction, pathing, and upgrade effects remain those of Vanilla Vehicles Expanded.
[*] [b]Visual scope:[/b] Corvega currently ships no vehicle retextures; the source mods' models and textures remain visible under the new identities.
[/list]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*] None. Install one or more supported vehicle mods for Corvega to have content to convert.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3014906877][b]Vanilla Vehicles Expanded[/b][/url] - Chryslus Highwayman and Corvega APC, Prowler, Roadkill and Roadrunner branding.
[*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3047892432][b]Vanilla Vehicles Expanded - Tier 3[/b][/url] - Corvega Lightning branding.
[*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3302208420][b]Vanilla Vehicles Expanded - Upgrades[/b][/url] - Fallout-style vehicle upgrade names.
[*] All three integrations are conditional and independent.
[/list]

[h2]Development Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*] Corvega is a localization and branding module. It does not alter vehicle statistics or add a second upgrade framework.
[*] All three vehicle integrations are optional and isolated from one another through conditional LoadFolders.
[*] Existing constructed vehicles may retain cached names in some menus until respawned or rebuilt.
[*] [url=https://github.com/BavarianBarbarian21/Fallout-Immersion-Project]Source code and development repository[/url]
[*] This mod is part of the Fallout Immersion Project (FIP) - a modular overhaul inspired by Fallout's lore. FIP is not affiliated with the Fallout Collaboration Project (FCP), but is designed to complement it. [url=https://discordapp.com/channels/644497305650593793/1414315188483657869]FIP channel in the FCP Discord[/url]
[/list]

[h2]Fallout Immersion Project[/h2]
FIP is a modular RimWorld overhaul inspired by Fallout. Install only the parts you want, or combine them for the complete conversion. FIP is independent from the Fallout Collaboration Project, but is built to work alongside it.
