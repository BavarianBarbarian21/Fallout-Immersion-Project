[h1]Fallout Immersion Project - RobCo[/h1]
[i]"Protect and serve." - Protectron[/i]

[url=https://ko-fi.com/misterhouse][img]https://i.imgur.com/YUpX4Up.png[/img][/url]

RobCo turns Biotech's Mechanitor route into a Fallout robot campaign. Start with a mechanic and four refurbished service bots, build a workforce of specialized machines, fight your way through increasingly dangerous robot signals, and recover the Platinum Chip to unlock one of five endgame projects.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]34 Fallout machines and synthetics:[/b] the roster ranges from Eyebots, Protectrons, Mister Handy models, Cyberdogs and Robobrains to Assaultrons, Sentry Bots, Securitrons, Synths, Roboscorpions and unique heavy prototypes.
[*] [b]Robots that earn their place in a colony:[/b] Constructrons build, Factotrons craft, Utilitrons mine, Collectrons haul, Mister Farmhands tend crops, Mister Handies cook and clean, and Miss Nannies provide medical care. Combat models bring their own weapons and battlefield roles.
[*] [b]A complete Mechanitor conversion:[/b] the starting scenario becomes [b]The RobCo Mechanic[/b], hostile mechanoid raids use Fallout robots, and gestators, scanners, subcores, implants, signal chips and control equipment receive new RobCo identities and artwork.
[*] [b]Four escalating robot hunts:[/b] assemble RobCo communications equipment to call down a crashed Vertibot, the Hydra, the Sheepsquatch and the Deathbot. Each fight replaces Biotech's standard Mechanitor boss progression with a distinct Fallout encounter.
[*] [b]The Platinum Chip quest:[/b] intercept a courier, follow the recovered route to a defended pre-War vault, and choose between five leads tied to the CEO, the People's Republic, the Calculator, the Think Tank or the University.
[*] [b]Endgame recovery projects:[/b] vault expeditions yield ruined prototypes rather than ready-made rewards. Research their technology and restore Liberty Prime, a War Machine, the Pacificator, a giant Roboscorpion queen or a Courser at the RobCo restoration gantry.
[*] [b]Purpose-built combat systems:[/b] robots use integrated lasers, miniguns, rocket salvos, flamers, sonic weapons and stealth fields. Eyebots improve nearby shooters, War Machines strengthen Liberators, and Liberty Prime can throw uranium-fed mini-nukes.
[*] [b]Full audiovisual treatment:[/b] RobCo includes 275 textures together with dedicated robot voices, weapon sounds, effects, buildings and interface art. This is new playable content, not a name-only replacement pack.
[/list]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Biotech DLC[/b] - Required for Mechanitors, robot control, gestation and xenotypes.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2009463077][b]Harmony[/b][/url] - Enables the University branch and restoration of its recovered Courser.
[*] [b]Anomaly DLC[/b] - Uses Anomaly's burner effects for compatible RobCo flamers when installed.
[*] [b]Royalty DLC[/b] - Replaces robots found in mech capsules, assemblers and drop beacons with RobCo models when installed.
[*] Optional integrations are loaded only when their source is active; none of them is required beyond Biotech.
[/list]

[h2]Fallout Immersion Project[/h2]
FIP is a modular RimWorld overhaul inspired by Fallout. Install only the parts you want, or combine them for the complete conversion. FIP is independent from the Fallout Collaboration Project, but is built to work alongside it.
