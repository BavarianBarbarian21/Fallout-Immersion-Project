FIP XENOTYPE SPAWN AUDIT
========================

Audit date: 25 July 2026
Game version: RimWorld 1.6.4871 rev591

Scope
-----

- RimWorld Core and all official DLC XML.
- RimWorld's Assembly-CSharp.dll, including quest-root and pawn-generation code.
- All locally installed Fallout Collaboration Project (FCP) 1.6 modules.
- Locally installed Workshop mods, including Vanilla Expanded.
- Existing FIP patches that are active only when WestTek is loaded.

The audit separates three mechanisms because they behave differently:

1. A forced xenotype or an exact PawnKind always produces that xenotype.
2. A PawnKind xenotypeSet adds its chances after the faction xenotypeSet.
   Replacing only the FactionDef therefore does not remove local PawnKind chances.
3. A CharacterDef or custom C# stock generator can bypass both faction and
   PawnKind selection.


RESULT
------

There is no current Vanilla 1.6 quest code that explicitly asks for Highmate,
Genie, Hussar, Yttakin, Neanderthal, Pigskin, Impid, Dirtmole, or Waster by
XenotypeDef name.

In particular, no Empire quest contains a hard-coded Highmate reward. Highmates
can appear in Empire-related content because several PawnKinds add a Highmate
chance. WestTek currently replaces XenotypeDef "Highmate" with Snuffy, so any
such roll becomes a Snuffy, but the underlying event is probabilistic rather
than an explicit Highmate reward.

Two Vanilla Biotech quests do explicitly create the Sanguophage PawnKind:

- QuestNode_Root_SanguophageMeetingHost
- QuestNode_Root_SanguophageShip

The Sanguophage starting scenario also explicitly assigns Sanguophage to two
starting pawns and Baseliner to one starting pawn.


VANILLA AND DLC: LOCAL PAWNKIND CHANCES
---------------------------------------

These chances remain relevant even when a faction's xenotypeSet was replaced,
because RimWorld adds PawnKind chances to faction chances.

Core:

- SlaveBase / Slave: Highmate 3%
- SpaceRefugeeBase: Highmate 3%
- TraderBase / Town_Trader: Highmate 4%

Royalty:

- RefugeeBase / Refugee: Highmate 2.5%
- Slave_Empire: Highmate 5%
- Empire_Common_Trader: Highmate 5%
- Other Empire commoner, soldier, guard, and noble PawnKinds locally mix
  Neanderthal, Hussar, Genie, Sanguophage, and Starjack at role-dependent
  probabilities.

FIP status for Royalty:

- Whitespring_EmpirePawnKinds_WestTek.xml replaces all current Vanilla Empire
  PawnKind sets. Lower-status roles use Atlantic/Baseliner; elite and government
  roles use Pure Humans/Vault Dwellers.
- RefugeeBase's own Highmate chance is not currently removed by the
  OutlanderRefugee FactionDef patch.

Odyssey:

- TradersGuild_Heavy: Hussar 60%
- TradersGuildEliteBase and its descendants: Hussar 60%
- TradersGuild faction: Hussar 5%, Genie 10%, Starjack 25%
- Salvagers faction: Hussar 5%, Genie 10%, Starjack 10%

FIP status for Odyssey:

- H&H Tools replaces the TradersGuild and Salvagers faction xenotypeSets.
- The two local TradersGuild PawnKind sets still add their 60% Hussar chance and
  therefore remain an unhandled path.

Anomaly:

- HoraxianBase: Hussar 5%, Dirtmole 5%, Genie 2.5%, Neanderthal 2.5%
- MutantBase: Dirtmole 10%, Hussar 5%, plus smaller Waster, Pigskin,
  Neanderthal, Impid, Genie, Yttakin, and Starjack chances.

FIP status for Anomaly:

- H&H Tools adds a WestTek faction set to HoraxCult.
- The local HoraxianBase set is still added on top and remains unhandled.
- MutantBase is also still unhandled.

Ideology:

- Beggars and Pilgrims have faction-level chances for several Vanilla
  xenotypes, including Highmate 2.5%.
- H&H Tools replaces Pilgrims when Greenway and WestTek are active.
- Beggars are not currently replaced.

Biotech xenotype factions:

- TribeRoughNeanderthal: Neanderthal weight 999
- PirateYttakin: Yttakin weight 999
- TribeSavageImpid: Impid weight 999
- OutlanderRoughPig: Pigskin weight 999
- PirateWaster: primarily Waster, with smaller Vanilla xenotype chances

H&H Tools hides these faction templates from world creation by default, but
their exact/weighted xenotype behavior still exists if the filter is disabled
or they are generated by another mechanism.


VANILLA EXPANDED AND OTHER INSTALLED WORKSHOP MODS
--------------------------------------------------

Vanilla Factions Expanded - Empire:

- Its extra noble and specialist PawnKinds define local Neanderthal, Hussar,
  Genie, Sanguophage, or Baseliner sets.
- Whitespring_VFEEmpirePawnKinds_WestTek.xml already replaces all current VFE
  Empire role sets, including Absolver and Techfriar.

Vanilla Factions Expanded - Settlers:

- Settler faction defs add Neanderthal, Hussar, Dirtmole, Impid, and sometimes
  Pigskin chances.
- These faction templates are hidden by the H&H Tools world-creation filter.

Vanilla Factions Expanded - Medieval 2:

- Clan factions add Yttakin 15% and Hussar 25%.
- Kingdom and Merchant Guild factions add Neanderthal 2.5%, Hussar 10%, and
  Dirtmole 15%.
- The replaced kingdoms and clans are hidden by the H&H Tools filter.

Ancient Urban Ruins:

- Several generated enemy/boss PawnKinds explicitly use Baseliner 100%.
- Boss PawnKinds also define Neanderthal 3%, with one adding Hussar 40%.
- These are local PawnKind sets and are not currently patched by FIP.

No additional installed Workshop assembly contained an obvious reference to
ForcedXenotype, SetXenotype, XenotypeDef, or xenotypeSet beyond the general
Vanilla Expanded Framework inheritance support. This does not rule out a mod
that constructs a Def name indirectly at runtime, but no such case was found.


FALLOUT COLLABORATION PROJECT
-----------------------------

Named characters:

FCP CharacterDefs explicitly force Baseliner for 167 named characters:

- Brotherhood of Steel: 40
- Caesar's Legion: 17
- Enclave: 10
- Great Khans: 10
- NCR and Powder Gangers: 76
- FCP Tools: 3
- Wastelanders: 11

These CharacterDefs are used by FCP settlement characters, group generation,
traders, and unique-character content. They bypass FactionDef xenotypeSets, so
the existing H&H Tools faction patches cannot turn those named characters into
regional WestTek humans.

FCP slave/trader generator:

FCP.Core.StockGenerator_PawnsWithXenotype passes a ForcedXenotype to RimWorld.
If the XML does not specify xenotypeDef, its C# default is Baseliner. Current
uses include Brotherhood, Legion, and NCR slave traders as well as several FCP
robot trader entries. The human slave entries therefore force Baseliner instead
of consulting their faction set.

FCP ghoul PawnKinds:

- FCP_WastelanderkindBase: 20% FCP Ghoul, otherwise Baseliner
- FCP_GhoulkindBase: 100% FCP Ghoul
- FCP_Glowing_OnekindBase: 100% FCP Glowing One
- Feral Ghoul PawnKind: 100% FCP Feral Ghoul
- Wastelander Ghoul PawnKind: 100% FCP Ghoul
- Glowing One PawnKind: 100% FCP Glowing One

These are intentional Fallout xenotypes, not Vanilla leakage.


FIP'S OWN FORCED CASES
----------------------

- WestTek's Super Mutant scenario explicitly starts with first- and
  second-generation Super Mutants.
- WestTek mutation/FEV code intentionally assigns WestTek xenotypes.
- RobCo creation mechanics intentionally assign their Synth/Think Tank
  definitions where appropriate.


RECOMMENDED FOLLOW-UP
---------------------

To eliminate only unintended Vanilla xenotype leakage without breaking
intentional factions:

1. Remove or replace the local xenotypeSet on Core SlaveBase,
   SpaceRefugeeBase, TraderBase, and Royalty RefugeeBase in an H&H Tools +
   WestTek load folder.
2. Replace Odyssey's TradersGuild_Heavy and TradersGuildEliteTierBase sets.
3. Replace Anomaly's HoraxianBase and MutantBase sets.
4. Decide whether Beggars, hidden Biotech xenotype factions, VFE Settlers,
   VFE Medieval 2, and Ancient Urban Ruins should remain Vanilla-styled when
   manually enabled.
5. Add optional FCP patches for the 167 CharacterDefs and the three human
   slave stock generators if they should use regional WestTek populations.

These changes should be made per source mod/load folder. A global replacement
of XenotypeDefs such as Highmate -> Snuffy works, but it changes every use of
that XenotypeDef and hides which source still requested it.
