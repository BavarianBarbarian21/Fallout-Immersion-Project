FCP WEAPON CATALOGUE AND FIP FACTION DISTRIBUTION PLAN
======================================================

Audit date: 24 July 2026

Scope
-----
Installed FCP weapon content:
- FCP Ballistic Weapons
- FCP Energy Weapons
- FCP Melee Weapons
- FCP Unarmed Weapons
- FCP Brotherhood of Steel
- FCP Enclave
- FCP NCR

H&H Tools contains optional compatibility names for FCP PRC and United States,
but neither faction mod is present in the local FCP installation audited here.
They therefore cannot be given verified PawnKind mappings yet. If either mod is
installed later, Donaustahl should add a conditional doctrine profile after
examining its actual FactionDef and PawnKindDefs.

Faction scope:
- vanilla and DLC factions not removed by the H&H Tools world-creation filter
- locally installed Vanilla Expanded factions not removed by that filter
- non-abstract FIP factions
- installed FCP factions

This is a catalogue and implementation proposal. No weapon distribution patch
has been applied yet.


1. VERIFIED COUNTS AND SOURCE ISSUE
===================================

Physical ThingDef weapon declarations:
- FCP Ballistic Weapons: 100
- FCP Energy Weapons: 35
- FCP Melee Weapons: 20
- FCP Unarmed Weapons: 6
- FCP Brotherhood of Steel: 4
- FCP Enclave: 5
- FCP NCR: 5
- Total declarations: 175

Unique defNames: 174

Reason for the difference:
FCP_Gun_Anti_Material_Rifle is declared independently by both FCP Ballistic
Weapons and FCP NCR. The labels even differ slightly ("Anti-Materiel Rifle"
versus "Anti-Material Rifle"). This should be resolved before final balancing.
The Ballistic Weapons definition should be canonical; NCR should reference it
and add an NCR loadout tag rather than declaring a second ThingDef.

Rarity metadata across the 175 declarations:
- Common: 37
- Rare: 92
- Unique: 37
- no direct rarity value: 9

FCP rarity is not sufficient for faction distribution. "Rare" includes both
ordinary military rifles and top-tier plasma weapons. The proposed FIP tags
therefore describe technology/access separately from item rarity.


2. PROPOSED TWO-AXIS CATEGORIZATION
===================================

Every weapon receives:

A) one functional role
----------------------
- FIPW_Role_Melee
- FIPW_Role_Unarmed
- FIPW_Role_Sidearm
- FIPW_Role_SMG
- FIPW_Role_Shotgun
- FIPW_Role_Rifle
- FIPW_Role_Assault
- FIPW_Role_Sniper
- FIPW_Role_Heavy
- FIPW_Role_Flame
- FIPW_Role_Laser
- FIPW_Role_Plasma
- FIPW_Role_Gauss
- FIPW_Role_Pulse

B) one availability pool
------------------------
- FIPW_T0_Improvised
  Scrap, primitive, homemade, and very low-value weapons.

- FIPW_T1_Wasteland
  Civilian arms, hunting guns, common pre-war sidearms, and frontier weapons.

- FIPW_T2_Service
  Police and military weapons suitable for organized armed forces.

- FIPW_T3_Advanced
  Advanced automatic, heavy, power-armor, and conventional laser weapons.

- FIPW_T4_Elite
  Plasma, Gauss, pulse, and the most restricted faction technology.

- FIPW_Unique
  Named one-of-a-kind weapons. Never included in a random PawnKind pool.

Optional signature tags are added alongside the availability tag:
- FIPW_Signature_BoS
- FIPW_Signature_Enclave
- FIPW_Signature_NCR
- FIPW_Signature_Ranger

Existing FCP role tags should remain. The new FIP tags supplement them and
provide the lore/access layer that the current tags do not express. Donaustahl
PawnKinds should select the new FIP availability tags, not the broad legacy FCP
role tags; otherwise a named Unique weapon could leak back into a random pool.

4. DOCTRINE PROFILES
====================

Percentages below are target composition, not direct RimWorld XML weights.
RimWorld chooses equipment through PawnKind weaponTags and weaponMoney. Exact
ratios require separate PawnKinds/group-maker weights; ordinary tag unions only
define what is possible.

TRIBAL
------
- 60% vanilla primitive weapons (bows, spears, clubs)
- 30% FCP T0 melee
- 10% FCP T0 firearms
- leader-only chance for T1 firearms
- no T2+, laser, plasma, Gauss, or pulse

RAIDER_CRUDE
------------
- 25% T0 melee
- 40% T0 firearms
- 30% T1
- 5% T2, reserved for fighters/leaders
- exactly 0% T3/T4 and 0% energy

SETTLER
-------
- 10% T0 melee
- 15% T0 firearms
- 60% T1
- 15% T2 for guards, hunters, and leaders
- no plasma/Gauss/pulse; no heavy weapons

ROUGH_SETTLER
-------------
- 10% T0
- 50% T1
- 35% T2
- 5% T3 for leaders only
- no T4

CARAVAN_COMPANY
---------------
- 10% T0 melee
- 60% T1
- 25% T2
- 5% T3 for caravan guards
- no T4

VAULT
-----
- 10% T1
- 55% T2
- 25% conventional laser/flame
- 10% T3
- plasma only for an explicitly elite Vault encounter

MILITARY
--------
- 10% T1
- 60% T2
- 20% T3
- 10% conventional laser
- T4 only in a faction-specific elite profile

DESERTER / CIRCLE OF STEEL
--------------------------
- 10% T1
- 50% T2
- 20% T3
- 15% conventional/advanced laser
- 5% rare plasma/Gauss for elite units

BROTHERHOOD
-----------
- initiates: T2 ballistic and conventional laser
- knights: T2/T3 ballistic, laser, signature Crusader pistol
- paladins: T3, advanced laser, rare T4 plasma/Gauss
- heavy/power-armored roles: heavy weapons and signature BoS weapons
- ordinary members never receive Unique weapons

ENCLAVE
-------
- 10% T2 ballistic
- 20% conventional laser
- 20% T3 advanced/heavy laser
- 30% T4 plasma/Gauss/pulse
- 20% Enclave signature weapons
- heavy weapons only on heavy/power-armored PawnKinds

ENCLAVE_REMNANT
---------------
- 20% T2 ballistic
- 30% conventional laser
- 20% T3
- 20% T4
- 10% Enclave signature weapons
- less perfectly equipped than the full Enclave

NCR
---
- troopers: Service Rifle, T1 sidearms, and T2 service weapons
- heavy troopers: T2/T3 automatic and heavy ballistic weapons
- veterans: Marksman Carbine, Battle Rifle, sniper weapons
- Rangers: Ranger Sequoia, NCR Hunting Rifle, Anti-Materiel Rifle
- captured laser chance only for elite units; no normal plasma pool

LEGION
------
- recruits: T0 melee and primitive weapons
- legionaries: scrap machetes, spears, T1 pistols/rifles
- veterans: T2 ballistic and industrial melee
- commanders: very small captured T2/T3 chance
- no general laser/plasma/Gauss/pulse pool

GREAT_KHANS
-----------
- T0/T1 pistols, SMGs, caravan/sawn-off/hunting shotguns, lever rifles
- industrial melee
- rare T2 rifles for veterans
- no general energy pool

POWDER_GANGERS
---------------
- T0 pipe weapons and melee
- T1 revolvers, shotguns, varmint/hunting rifles
- very small T2 chance for bosses
- no energy or heavy weapons

GHOUL_SETTLEMENT
----------------
- T0/T1 wasteland arms
- somewhat more pre-war T1/T2 weapons than a tribal settlement
- no automatic technology bonus merely for being ghouls

SUPER_MUTANT
------------
- rebar clubs, bumper swords, sledgehammers, Super Sledges
- T2 assault weapons
- T3 machine guns/miniguns for heavy roles
- rare laser/plasma only for masters or explicitly advanced armies
- no delicate low-power sidearm pool for ordinary super mutants

WENDIGO_CULT
-------------
- melee-forward T0/T2 industrial weapons
- silenced T1/T2 sidearms and SMGs for hunters
- no random plasma/Gauss/pulse weapons

MOTHMAN_CULT
-------------
- T0 melee, hunting rifles, shotguns, and limited T1 firearms
- holy/hostile cult fighters may receive limited T2
- no advanced energy pool

6. DONAUSTAHL IMPLEMENTATION DESIGN
===================================

Why PawnKinds must be patched
-----------------------------

FactionDef does not directly choose a weapon. Its pawnGroupMakers choose
PawnKindDefs; each PawnKindDef then filters weapons through weaponTags and
weaponMoney. Patching only FactionDef would not prevent a Nuevo Texico raider
from receiving an inappropriate weapon.

Recommended structure
---------------------

Add optional Donaustahl load folders:
- LoadFolders/FCP_BallisticWeapons
- LoadFolders/FCP_EnergyWeapons
- LoadFolders/FCP_MeleeWeapons
- LoadFolders/FCP_UnarmedWeapons
- LoadFolders/FCP_BOS
- LoadFolders/FCP_Enclave
- LoadFolders/FCP_NCR
- LoadFolders/FCP_Factions
- LoadFolders/HHTools_FCPWeapons
- LoadFolders/Vanilla_FCPWeapons
- LoadFolders/VFEEmpire_FCPWeapons
- LoadFolders/VFEDeserters_FCPWeapons
- LoadFolders/VFEMedieval2_FCPWeapons
- LoadFolders/VFETribals_FCPWeapons
- LoadFolders/VGeneticsE_FCPWeapons

Use LoadFolders.xml package checks instead of broad PatchOperationFindMod
wrappers. Donaustahl's About.xml must load after every FCP weapon/faction
package and every target FIP/VE faction mod it patches.

Verified FCP packageIds for those checks:
- Rick.FCP.BallisticWeapons
- Rick.FCP.EnergyWeapons

Patch stages
------------

1) Weapon classification
   Add the new role, tier, Unique, and signature tags to FCP ThingDefs.

2) Unique isolation
   Ensure all 37 Unique weapons have only FIPW_Unique for availability and are
   never added to generic PawnKind tags. Existing personal/story tags can stay.

3) PawnKind profiles
   Replace broad vanilla tags on faction combat PawnKinds with curated FIP
   availability tags. Patch civilians, hunters, fighters, leaders, traders,
   heavy troops, and specialists separately.

4) Faction-specific cloning where required
   If two factions share one PawnKind but require different doctrines, clone
   that PawnKind in Donaustahl and replace only the appropriate
   pawnGroupMakers option. Do not globally alter a shared PawnKind and thereby
   leak Enclave weapons into another faction.

5) Weapon budgets
   Set weaponMoney ranges so T3/T4 weapons actually fit only elite PawnKinds.
   Tags prevent forbidden technology; budgets control quality and frequency.

6) Heavy/species restrictions
   Keep miniguns, shoulder weapons, K9000, and power-armor rifles out of normal
   human pools. Only appropriate heavy, power-armored, Cyberdog, or super-mutant
   PawnKinds receive their special tags.

7) Preserve fallback weapons
   Every patched PawnKind needs at least one valid weapon in every supported
   mod combination. A melee fallback prevents "no usable weapon" generation
   errors when only part of FCP is active.

Do not use direct percentage claims in a simple weaponTags list
---------------------------------------------------------------

weaponTags form an allowed set; they do not express 40/30/20/10 weights.
Approximate the profile through role-specific PawnKinds and weaponMoney.
For strict percentages, use separate PawnKind variants with explicit
pawnGroupMakers weights or a small, transparent generation component.


7. REQUIRED VALIDATION BEFORE RELEASE
=====================================

1) Resolve the duplicate FCP_Gun_Anti_Material_Rifle source.
2) Parse all Donaustahl XML and verify every XPath against the intended mod.
3) Build a loaded-Def audit containing each weapon's final FIP tags.
4) Spawn at least 20 raid groups and 20 trader groups for every patched faction.
5) Check leaders, traders, hunters, ordinary fighters, and heavy units
   separately.
6) Assert forbidden pools:
   - Nuevo Texico Raider: zero energy/T3/T4/Unique
   - tribes: zero advanced energy
   - ordinary settlers: zero plasma/Gauss/pulse/heavy
   - ordinary NCR troopers: zero plasma and no Ranger-only sidearms
   - non-Brotherhood: zero BoS signature weapons
   - non-Enclave: zero Enclave signature weapons
   - every random group: zero Unique weapons
7) Repeat with each optional FCP weapon module disabled to verify fallbacks.
8) Test both raids and traders; RimWorld may use different group makers and
   PawnKinds for the two.
